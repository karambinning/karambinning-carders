Opening
Open early through to late, seven days a week. Pop in — the door is usually open.
Getting here
A short walk south of the Southgate, on the London Road as it heads out of town. Buses on the London Road route stop close by.
Parking
On-street parking along London Road and the side streets, subject to local restrictions.
Accessibility
Step at the door — staff are happy to bring an item out to the pavement if that’s easier; just give us a wave.

Walk down London Road towards the Southgate — you’ll see us at number 128.
