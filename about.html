A note from the proprietors

About the shop.

Carders · News & Off Licence · South Lynn

Carders is a family-run newsagent and off licence on London Road in South Lynn, King’s Lynn. A small shop, run by the Carder family, that has quietly kept the neighbourhood supplied with the papers, the lottery, a bottle of wine and the daily essentials for years.

There is nothing fancy about a proper corner shop, and we don’t pretend otherwise. What we try to do is keep the shelves stocked with the things people actually need, open the door early, lock it late, and treat the people who come through it the way we’d want to be treated ourselves.

South Lynn has changed in plenty of ways over the years, but the basic job of a neighbourhood shop hasn’t. Someone needs a paper. Someone’s run out of milk. A regular wants a scratchcard and a chat about the football. A new face wants directions to the Southgate. We try to be useful in all of those small, daily ways.

If you’re local, you’ll likely have been in already. If you’re new to the area, walk down London Road towards the Southgate and you’ll see us at number 128. The door is open.

The papers in the morning, a bottle in the evening, and a kind word in between — that is the entire job.

What you’ll find inside

A small shop, properly stocked.

Daily papers and the local weeklies. A magazine rack worth browsing. A respectable off-licence shelf — wine, beer, cider and spirits, with familiar names and a few worth trying. Bread, milk, the children’s after-school snacks, the early-morning bacon-roll essentials, the late-night running-out-of-coffee rescue.

Lottery and scratchcards at the counter. Phone top-ups for the major networks. Greetings cards and stationery for the last-minute occasions. The full corner-shop list, kept honest and kept fresh.

If there is something you’d like us to stock and we don’t, ask at the counter — that is how shops like ours stay useful.
