Departments of the shop

What we stock.

The full corner-shop list · arranged by aisle

A proper newsagent and off licence keeps a surprising amount on the shelves for the size of the room. Here is what you can expect to find when you push the door open at 128 London Road.

I
News & print

Newspapers & magazines

The morning’s nationals, the local titles and the weekend supplements. A magazine rack covering news, sport, hobbies, motoring, home, fashion, telly listings, puzzles and children’s comics.

II
Off licence

Wine, beer, cider & spirits

A friendly off-licence shelf chosen for the South Lynn table — familiar bottles, cold beers and ciders for the weekend, a sensible spirits selection, and a couple of nicer wines worth taking home.

III
Daily essentials

Bread, milk & the basics

The running-out-of essentials — bread, milk, eggs, butter, tea, coffee, sugar, tins, pasta, cereal and the small store-cupboard things you need at half past nine on a Tuesday.

IV
Sweets & snacks

Confectionery & soft drinks

Crisps, chocolate, sweets, ice creams in the freezer, soft drinks across the chiller and the lunchtime grab-and-go bits for the school run, the work van and the riverside walk.

V
At the counter

National Lottery & scratchcards

Lottery terminal at the till for the main draws and the EuroMillions, with a full rack of scratchcards and the usual quiet hope that this is the week.

VI
Mobile & bills

Top-ups & PayPoint

Phone top-ups for the major mobile networks, and where signage shows, services for paying household bills, utility top-ups and small everyday paperwork.

VII
Last-minute

Greetings cards & stationery

A rack of cards for the birthdays, anniversaries, new babies, sympathies and good-lucks that always seem to come round when you’ve forgotten. Pens, envelopes, wrap and the basics alongside.

VIII
Behind the counter

Tobacco & vape

Cigarettes, rolling tobacco, papers and the regulated counter range — ID checks the standard. Vape products subject to availability and local rules.

If you’d like us to start stocking something, just ask at the counter — that is how a corner shop earns its keep.
